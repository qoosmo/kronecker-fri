\section{Verification Status}\label{app:verification}

This appendix records, for each result, how it is established.
\emph{Proved} means proved in this paper; \emph{from~\cite{Mkh26}} means that the statement and its proof are reproduced from the companion paper, whose Lean~4 project formalises most of these statements, sometimes in a slightly different form (for instance with a sumcheck and a final table instead of a final polynomial), with the exceptions of \cref{lem:descent,lem:merkle}; \emph{adapted} means that the proof follows one of~\cite{Mkh26} with changes; \emph{quoted} means that the result is cited and not reproved.
\emph{Tested} refers to the Rust test suite and examples of \cref{sec:impl:tests}, which check the statement on random instances; tests are evidence, not proofs.
No result of this paper is formalised in Lean yet; a formalisation of \cref{sec:kernel,sec:fold,sec:scheme,sec:sound} is in preparation.
