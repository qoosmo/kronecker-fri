Kronecker-FRI

A hash-based multilinear polynomial commitment whose evaluation proofs use no sumcheck: an evaluation is one coefficient of a product, checked by plain FRI.

The protocol

Commit once, open with one oracle and FRI

The prover splits X Uf Kz = A + v XN + XN+1H with A, H of degree < N. If such polynomials exist, v must be the right value (Lemma 4.12).

Commit

Evaluate Uf on a smooth multiplicative domain of order M = 4N (one NTT) and publish a Merkle root, one leaf per fibre {±ξ}.

Send one opening oracle

The prover commits to A only. The verifier never receives H: at each query it computes h(ξ) = (ξw(ξ)Kz(ξ) − wA(ξ) − vξN) / ξN+1, a virtual word.

Batch

A random β merges the commitment, wA and h into one word. If the claimed value is wrong, h contains the term c/ξ, which agrees with any low-degree codeword on at most N points.

Fold and query

Plain FRI with the kernel fold, down to a final polynomial, then κ queries. The verifier is an FRI verifier plus O(n) field operations per query. No sumcheck.

Results

Proved in the paper

Self-contained proofs in the unique-decoding regime. The only external result about codes is the correlated-agreement theorem of Ben-Sasson, Carmon, Ishai, Kopparty and Saraf.

Thm 4.5 · Lem 4.10, 4.12

Extraction and opening identities

f(z) = [XN−1] UfKz; the split into opening polynomials, and why the factor X makes the degree bound N sufficient.

Thm 5.24

Folding test for arbitrary words

Fibre distance, good challenges, witness sets and the codeword chain, stated for any prover so they serve as the round-by-round state.

Lem 7.4 · Thm 7.8

Batching lemma and soundness

Correlated agreement for curves of degree 2 plus the identity lemma, through the virtual word. Error below 3M/|𝔽| + (1−δ)κ.

Thm 7.11 · Cor 7.12

Knowledge and binding

Round-by-round knowledge soundness with a decoding extractor, and evaluation binding.

Thm 8.5 · Cor 8.6

Post-quantum

Straightline knowledge soundness of the compiled scheme in the quantum random oracle model (Chiesa–Di–Hu–Zheng), for one opening: εext < 2−31.8 at 264 queries.

Thm 11.2

Batched openings

m polynomials at one point for the cost of one opening, with error (m−1)M/|𝔽| + εKF, in fibre distance.

Scope

Why Kronecker-FRI

Offered

  • No sumcheck; the verifier is FRI plus local work.
  • A one-step soundness argument on top of the folding test.
  • Built from components that FRI-based systems already have, including higher-arity folding and Merkle caps.
  • Transparent and hash-based; no trusted setup.

Parameters

  • 100 bits: 𝔽q², ρ = 1/4, κ = 148 — 390 KiB salted at n = 20.
  • 128 bits: 𝔽q⁴, κ = 189 — 586 KiB.
  • Post-quantum: 𝔽q⁴, κ = 248, salted — 755 KiB.
